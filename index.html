whoami?

I am a first (going to second) year Computer Science Ph.D. student at the University of Maryland, College Park, advised by Prof. Yizheng Chen.

My research interests include AI Security, Machine Learning, and Natural Language Processing.

I have a master's at CMU in Natural Language Processing from the LTI, School of Computer Science. (Master of Science in Intelligent Information Systems)

Before that, I was a Research Fellow at Microsoft Research India, working with Prof. Monojit Choudhury, who is now at MBZUAI.

Full list of advisors here.


I have worked on two kinds of problems: AI Ethics, involving moral and cultural biases, and AI Safety, including LLM Jailbreaks, and LLM4Code Security.
